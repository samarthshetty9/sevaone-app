# Connections

The demo has **no live external connections**. Everything here is what it imitates or came from.

**Source material**
- `~/Work/seva-one` (web portal): colours, logo, 1902 helpline and `Sakala & Sevasindhu Services` CSV, converted to `assets/data/catalog.json`. Read only; nothing links back to it.
- `Sevaone-Plan.pdf` (UMANG vs SevaOne comparison): features 1 to 21.
- UMANG app screenshots and App Store listing: feature reference only, not UI.

- Noto Sans and Noto Sans Kannada (SIL Open Font License), bundled in `assets/fonts`.

**What a real build would need:** DigiLocker, NIC Service Plus, Sakala and token status APIs, iPGRS, Aadhaar e-KYC and e-Sign, a payment gateway and utility department APIs, SMS gateway with DLT registration and short code (the app shows `56XXX` as a placeholder), IVR provider, WhatsApp Business, weather and AQI APIs, GramaOne / Karnataka One location data, push service (APNs/FCM), speech services, OCR and camera plugins, recorded ISL video.
